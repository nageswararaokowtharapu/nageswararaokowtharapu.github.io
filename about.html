The story behind the work

From engineering to questions about people and technology.

I'm Nageswara Rao Kowtharapu. My journey began in computer science, developed through software engineering and data science, and has increasingly drawn me toward the ways artificial intelligence influences how people perceive information, build trust, and make decisions.

THE FOUNDATION

Starting with computer science in India

I pursued a Bachelor of Technology in Computer Science and Engineering at Lovely Professional University in Punjab, completing my degree in 2021. That foundation introduced me to programming, technical problem-solving, and the value of translating complex ideas into working systems.

2021–2022

Learning through hands-on data engineering

During my Data Science Engineer internship at HCL Technologies, I worked with Python, Spark, and AWS-based data workflows. The experience exposed me to practical machine learning and natural language processing, including sentiment-analysis work, and showed me how data systems support real business questions.

2022–2023

Connecting analytics to customer behavior

At Wipro, I worked as a Data Scientist, analyzing customer and marketing-related data using Python, SQL, statistical models, and visualization. Projects involving regression, hypothesis testing, and campaign analysis strengthened my interest in what data can reveal about people’s choices.

GRADUATE STUDY

Expanding my perspective at Clark University

I pursued an MS in Information Technology at Clark University in Worcester, Massachusetts, graduating in 2024. Graduate study helped me deepen my technical foundations while considering how technology, organizations, and people interact.

2025–PRESENT

Building software and machine-learning systems

As a Software Engineer at Bit4Logic, my work focuses on Python applications, REST APIs, data pipelines, and machine-learning workflows. I enjoy building practical systems, but I'm equally interested in understanding how such systems affect the people who use them.

CURRENT RESEARCH DIRECTION

Exploring AI, marketing, and decision-making

My research interests center on quantitative marketing, consumer behavior, AI-driven personalization, digital advertising, consumer trust, and decision confidence. I have written about human–AI collaboration in advertising and continue developing research on personalization and consumer responses.

Looking ahead, I hope to deepen this work through doctoral research, combining computational methods with careful empirical questions about consumers and digital experiences.
